AI COACH · TRAINING PLANNING

You set the week. The coach plans every session.

ENDURE’s AI coach drafts your coming weeks against the targets you set, explains every session and picks today’s session each morning. The coach drafts, you decide: nothing lands in your calendar until you apply it.

  1. 01

    Week planner Beta

    Choose a week type for each coming week (Recovery, Maintain, Build or Focus) and set its TSS target. The load forecast rates every week before you ride it: On track, Borderline, Too little or Too much.

  2. 02

    Plan with coach Beta

    Tap Plan with coach and the coach drafts the coming weeks against your targets, aimed at your goal event if you have one. It explains the draft in a conversation: ask for a shorter Tuesday or a free Sunday and it revises it. Nothing lands in your calendar until you tap Apply.

  3. 03

    Every session explained

    Each planned session comes with the coach’s reasoning and two alternatives: a lighter one if your legs feel heavy, a harder one if you feel fresh. Swap in the session details.

    DAILY GUIDANCE

    Every morning the coach reads your check-in and your recent rides and picks today’s session, with the reason. When today is already planned, it may propose one change, and you accept or skip it. One tap starts the workout, and your smart trainer follows in ERG mode.
